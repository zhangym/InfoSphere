[[How to Self-educate with AI.excalidraw]]

提示词：

```
I want to self-educate on topics that help me solve the biggest problems I am currently facing. No shelf-help, no learning for the sake of learning. Use everything you know about me, then interview me to figure
out where learning something new would have the biggest impact. Then do a deep research project to build me a list of resources to learn from.
I believe in just-in-time learning, not just-in-case - only recommend things I can apply immediately. Don't give me the most popular resources. Give me the ones that fit my current struggles. Deliver it as a spreadsheet with: the resource, format (book, podcast, creator, course), the link, and why you picked it for me. If anything's unclear, ask before you start.
```

```
I want a daily brief on [your space  Al, business, finance].
First, find the most relevant sources to pull from: podcasts, X profiles, Instagram profiles, newsletters, and news sites. Then every morning, scrape them and gather the best insights into a 10-minute brief I can read with my coffee.
Only include the relevant things I'm learning right now - Cut the noise out. I don't need clickbait, I don't need distractions, I don't need things that repeat themselves.
```
